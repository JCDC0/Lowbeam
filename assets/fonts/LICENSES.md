# Fonts

Every font here is under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and is bundled with the game unchanged
(the CJK fonts split into unicode-range pieces by Fontsource). `tools/vendor-fonts.mjs` copies them
from `node_modules` and writes `fonts.css`.
